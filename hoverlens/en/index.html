01 / HOW IT WORKS

Hover. Tap Option.
Get the translation.

No copying. No switching windows. Keep your attention on the thought in front of you.

01

Point

Place your cursor over a word. HoverLens reads accessible text first, then tries OCR around the cursor when needed.

02

Tap

Tap and release Option. Prefer another gesture? Set a custom shortcut or turn on automatic hover.

Understand. Carry on.

Read definitions, translation, and pronunciation in a floating panel. Point to another word and tap Option to keep going.

02 / FEATURES

Dictionary. Translation.
OCR. Pronunciation.

Choose the dictionary, translation, and recognition sources that work for you.

A

Dictionary first. More when needed.

Look up meanings with the offline ECDICT English–Chinese dictionary or your enabled macOS dictionaries. By default, translations are requested when you expand them.

ECDICT needs an initial download of about 52 MB, then works offline.
B

Local translation. Or your own AI.

Use Apple translation on macOS 26+, or enter your own endpoint, model, and API key for an OpenAI-compatible service with streaming replies.

Apple translation requires supported language packs. Cloud provider charges may apply.
C

Words inside images, too.

When direct lookup fails, Apple Vision tries to recognize nearby text on your Mac. Try images, PDFs, and paused video frames. Baidu OCR is also available as an option.

Results depend on image clarity. Protected content may block capture.
D

Hear the word. Find its rhythm.

Use Apple’s on-device speech with the speaker button. Choose American or British English and adjust playback from 0.5–1.5× speed.

Matching installed voices work offline without an API key.

FAQ

A few things you might be wondering.

Which Macs are supported?+

The current v0.4.4 download supports Apple Silicon (M-series chips) on macOS 14 or later. An Intel build is not currently provided. Apple on-device translation additionally requires macOS 26+. Earlier systems can use offline and system dictionaries or compatible cloud translation.

Do I need to pay or bring an API key?+

HoverLens is free to download. Local OCR, system dictionaries, ECDICT, and on-device speech need no API key. Apple translation requires a supported system and language packs. Cloud translation and Baidu OCR need your own credentials; provider quotas and charges apply.

What does notarization mean for installation?+

This download is Developer ID signed. Both the app and DMG passed Apple notarization, have stapled tickets, and passed Gatekeeper checks. Follow macOS prompts when opening the app, then grant the lookup permissions. Notarization is Apple’s automated security check, rather than App Store review.

Does it translate entire articles or video subtitles?+

HoverLens focuses on word lookup around your cursor. OCR can try text in images or paused video, but the app does not provide full-article or live subtitle translation. Recognition depends on fonts, clarity, and whether the content allows capture.

Where are API keys stored?+

Credentials are stored in a permission-restricted local file on your Mac. They are currently unencrypted and do not use Keychain. No HoverLens developer account is required. Keep your service credentials secure.

How do I update or send feedback?+

Updates will be available on GitHub Releases. Download and replace the app manually. To report an issue, email the developer with your app and macOS versions.
